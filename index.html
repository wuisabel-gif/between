A COMMUNICATION AID FOR AUTISTIC PEOPLE

When the words are clear,
but the meaning isn't.

Between is for autistic people who want another way to examine an ambiguous message. It lays out the literal reading, a few plausible alternatives, and the clues that make the situation uncertain — so you can choose what feels useful and decide what to do next.

Start with the words

What the message literally says stays visible. An implied meaning should never erase the plain reading.

02

Keep more than one door open

People can be sarcastic, playful, tired, indirect, or simply literal. The interface makes room for that.

03

Choose a low-pressure next step

A clarifying question is often safer than guessing. Between offers a prompt, never an instruction.

A SMALL PROMISE

You do not have to
read between the lines alone.

Between is a user-controlled accessibility layer for digital conversation. It is designed for autistic people, private by default, careful about uncertainty, and always willing to say “there isn't enough information yet.”

between / an early product prototype Not a diagnosis. Not a mind reader. A tool for a second look.
